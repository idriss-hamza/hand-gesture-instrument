2. HAND ROTATION SOUND CONTROLS (FIST CLUTCH SYSTEM)
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
Rotate / tilt your hand clockwise or counter-clockwise in front of the camera:
NOTE: Rotary controls only activate when you form a CLOSED FIST!
When your hand is OPEN (or doing any other gesture), rotation has NO effect,
allowing you to comfortably return your hand to center (50% marker) while
keeping your dialed volume and pitch shift locked in place!

  [RIGHT HAND] -> PITCH SHIFT / BEND:
    * Close Fist and rotate to adjust pitch offset (-12 to +12 semitones).
    * Upright (0 deg): Natural base note (0 semitones).
    * Tilt Right (Clockwise): Shifts pitch UP (+1 to +12 semitones / sharps).
    * Tilt Left (Counter-Clockwise): Shifts pitch DOWN (-1 to -12 semitones / flats).
    * Open your hand to LOCK the pitch shift and play notes freely!

  [LEFT HAND] -> MASTER VOLUME DIAL:
    * Close Fist and rotate to adjust master volume (0% to 100%).
    * Tilt Left (-60 deg): 0% Volume (Mute).
    * Upright (0 deg): 65% Volume (Standard listening level).
    * Tilt Right (+60 deg): 100% Volume (Maximum loudness).
    * Open your hand to LOCK the volume (move hand back to 50% marker freely)!

3. MINIMALIST ON-SCREEN DISPLAY & MODES
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
  * Video & Hand Skeleton: Clean, unobstructed camera view with glowing landmarks.
  * Song Guide Mode [1]: Shows target note & symbol in vivid ORANGE. Guided play-along tutorial.
  * Free Instrument Mode [2/F]: Freely plays whatever gesture is shown on screen without guidance.
    Displays the detected note in vivid ORANGE with its symbol, pitch shift and volume controls.
  * Rotation Meters: Real-time Pitch Shift (Right Hand) and Volume % (Left Hand).
  * Digital Camera Top Cropping: Press [ or ] to dynamically fine-tune framing (-5% / +5%) to eliminate obstacles.
  * No distracting song highway or lyric clutter on the camera stream!
